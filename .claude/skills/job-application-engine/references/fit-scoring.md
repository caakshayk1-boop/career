# Fit Scoring Rubric

Score every job 0–100. The purpose is not to rank — it is to give the user permission to skip. A list where everything scores highly is a list that saves nobody any time.

## Weights

| Dimension | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Core skill match | 35 | Share of the JD's must-have hard skills evidenced in the Master Profile |
| Seniority alignment | 20 | Years and scope of responsibility against what the JD asks |
| Domain / industry match | 15 | Sector experience, or transferability if switching |
| Location & work mode | 15 | Against stated location and remote/hybrid/onsite preference |
| Salary alignment | 10 | Posted range against target; unlisted scores neutral (5) |
| Title trajectory | 5 | Is this a sensible next step from the current title |

## Bands

- **75–100 — Strong.** Apply. The CV will tailor well because the evidence exists.
- **55–74 — Worth a shot.** Apply if capacity allows. Name the specific weakness so the user chooses knowingly.
- **Below 55 — Skip.** Tailoring cannot close this gap without fabrication. State the reason in one line.

## Automatic caps

Some gaps cannot be argued away, and pretending otherwise wastes the user's week:

- Missing a hard requirement that is legally or structurally binding (a licence, a specific certification, a degree the role mandates, work authorisation) — **cap at 40**.
- Three or more years short of the stated minimum experience — **cap at 50**.
- Onsite role in a city the user ruled out — **cap at 30**.
- Any stated dealbreaker triggered — **cap at 25** and label it clearly.

## Writing the reason line

One line, specific, and about this job. Not "good match for your background".

Good: "Strong on channel sales and Tier-3 distribution, but they want SAP and your profile has none."
Good: "Everything lines up except they're onsite in Pune and you ruled out relocation."
Bad: "Solid fit, worth applying."

## Scoring transferable experience

When a user is switching domain, do not penalise the domain gap twice. Score domain on **transferability of the underlying capability**, not on sector label. Retail operations to D2C operations is high transferability. Retail operations to clinical research is not. Say which one it is.
